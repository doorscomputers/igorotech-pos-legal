Privacy Policy

App: Point of Sale (IgoroTech) · Package: com.anonymous.igorotechpos
Effective Date: 25 May 2026 · Last Updated: 25 May 2026

Summary: Point of Sale (IgoroTech) is an offline-first application. All your business data — sales, inventory, customers, reports — is stored only on your device. We do not operate any server that receives your data, we do not use analytics or advertising SDKs, and we do not sell or share your information with third parties.

2. Information We Collect

The app processes the following categories of data locally on your device. Except where explicitly noted, none of this information is transmitted to us or to any third party.

2.1 Device Identifiers (for license validation only)

To bind your purchased license to your device and enforce the 30-day trial period, the app reads:

  • Android ID — a non-resettable identifier scoped to this app's signing key (provided by expo-application)
  • Device brand, model name, and device name
  • Operating system name and version
  • App version

These values are combined and hashed (SHA-256) into a single device fingerprint. The fingerprint and the components above are stored in the local SQLite database on the device. They are not transmitted to any server.

If you choose to purchase a full license, you may share your device fingerprint with the seller (via WhatsApp or email that you open yourself) so a matching license key can be generated. You control what you send.

2.2 Business and Transaction Data

The app stores the data you enter to operate your business:

  • Products, categories, brands, suppliers, customers
  • Sales, purchases, returns, voids, exchanges
  • Inventory levels, stock movements, physical counts
  • Cash drawer activity, expenses, accounts payable/receivable
  • X-Reading, Z-Reading, and BIR-compliant electronic journal (eJournal) records
  • User accounts (username, hashed password, role)

All of this data is held in a local SQLite database (simplepos.db) on your device. It is not uploaded anywhere.

2.3 Bureau of Internal Revenue (BIR) Compliance Data

Where required by Philippine tax regulations (RR 7-2024, RR 11-2024, RA 9994, RA 10754, and related issuances), the app records customer Tax Identification Numbers (TIN), Senior Citizen / PWD ID numbers, business address, and similar information solely for the lawful purpose of issuing compliant invoices and maintaining the audit trail you are required to keep. This data remains on the device.

4. Data We Do NOT Collect

  • We do not use Google Analytics, Firebase Analytics, Crashlytics, Facebook SDK, or any third-party tracking SDK.
  • We do not show advertisements.
  • We do not access your contacts, photos, call logs, SMS, calendar, or microphone.
  • We do not track your geographic location.
  • We do not create an account on any cloud service on your behalf.

5. How Your Data Leaves the Device (only if you choose)

Data leaves your device only through actions you initiate:

  • Printing — sent over Bluetooth to a printer you pair.
  • CSV export / import — files you save to your device's storage or share via standard Android share targets.
  • Email reports — opens your device's email app with the report attached. You choose the recipient and decide whether to send.
  • "Contact Seller" / "Upgrade" buttons — open WhatsApp or email with a pre-filled message to the seller. You choose what to send.

We have no servers that receive any of these exports.

6. Trial Period and Licensing

The app may be downloaded free of charge and includes a 30-day trial. Trial state and license activation are stored on the device. No trial telemetry is sent to us. After the trial expires, continued use requires a license key obtained from the seller. The license key is generated from your device fingerprint and validated locally on the device.

7. Data Security

User passwords are hashed using SHA-256 before being stored. Your business data resides in your device's private app storage and is protected by Android's app sandbox. You are responsible for protecting physical access to the device and for backing up the SQLite database file if you wish to preserve your records.

8. Data Retention and Deletion

All data persists on the device until you delete it. To permanently erase all data, uninstall the app or use Android's "Clear Data" option for this app in system settings. Because we do not hold copies on any server, uninstalling the app permanently and irreversibly destroys the data.

9. Children's Privacy

This app is a business tool intended for adult merchants and their employees. It is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect information from children.

10. Your Rights (Philippine Data Privacy Act, RA 10173)

Because all personal data processed by the app is stored solely on your own device and never transmitted to us, you are the data controller of that data. You may at any time:

  • View, edit, or delete any record from within the app
  • Export your data via CSV
  • Erase everything by uninstalling the app or clearing app data

If, in any future version, we begin processing personal data on our own servers, this policy will be updated and your rights to access, correct, object, and request deletion under RA 10173 will be honored. Concerns may also be raised with the National Privacy Commission (https://www.privacy.gov.ph).

11. International Users

The app is offered primarily to merchants in the Philippines. Because no data leaves your device, no cross-border transfer of personal data occurs.
